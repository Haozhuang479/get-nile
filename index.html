Where does my data live?

我的数据在哪？

Nile keeps your brand's Memory on your Mac. Your agent reads and writes it; the app itself never changes it without you. Card details are entered on the billing page in your browser, never in the app.

品牌的 Memory 存在你的 Mac 上，由你的 agent 读写，app 本身不会背着你改。银行卡在浏览器的账单页输入，不会进 app。
